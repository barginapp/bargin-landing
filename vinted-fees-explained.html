Vinted fees explained

Vinted is free to sell on in the UK. The fees fall on the buyer, which matters a lot if you're buying on Vinted to resell.

The short version

  • Selling: no selling fee. If you list at £20 and it sells, you get £20.
  • Buying: the buyer pays the item price, plus Buyer Protection (usually 5% of the price + £0.70), plus postage.
  • Extras you choose: optional paid promotion like bumps or a wardrobe spotlight. Skip them until you know an item sells.

How Buyer Protection changes the maths

If you're sourcing stock on Vinted, Buyer Protection is part of your cost. On a £10 item it adds £1.20 (12%), and on a £4 item it adds £0.90, which is over 20%. That's why very cheap items are often worse flips than they look.

Example: you buy a jumper listed at £12.

£12.00 item + £1.30 Buyer Protection + £3.75 postage = £17.05 total cost.

If you resell it on Vinted for £30, you keep the full £30 (the buyer pays their own postage), so your profit is £12.95.

Selling on Vinted vs Depop

Depop also has no selling fee for UK sellers, but it does charge a payment processing fee of 2.9% + £0.30 on the item price plus postage. On a £30 sale that's about £1.17. Vinted has no equivalent seller charge, which is why many resellers buy and sell on Vinted.

Don't forget the costs that aren't fees

  • Postage to you when you buy.
  • Postage you pay if you offer free postage when selling.
  • Returns and "not as described" claims, which can wipe out a small profit.
  • Your time: photos, listing, packing and the drop-off.

Questions

Does Vinted charge sellers a fee?

No. UK sellers pay no selling fee on Vinted. You receive the full item price you listed.

How much is Vinted Buyer Protection?

It's usually 5% of the item price plus £0.70, paid by the buyer on top of the item price and postage.

Who pays postage on Vinted?

The buyer, in most cases. Sellers print a prepaid label, so postage isn't a cost to the seller unless they choose to offer free postage.
