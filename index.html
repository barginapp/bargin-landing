How it works

Three steps, no typing required

Bargin sits behind the share sheet you already use — it doesn't ask you to switch apps or copy anything by hand.

1

Share a listing

Spot something on Vinted or Depop mid-scroll. Tap Share → Bargin, right from the listing itself.

2

Get an instant verdict

Bargin checks real recent sold prices, works out UK fees and postage, and shows your potential profit or loss as a realistic range.

Track the flip

Log the offer, mark it bought, relisted, and resold — see your real profit build up over time, not just a one-off guess.

FAQ

Good to know

Which platforms does Bargin support?

Vinted and Depop for sourcing today, with resale verdicts benchmarked against real, recent UK sold prices. More platforms are on the roadmap.

Is this legal / financial advice?

No — Bargin gives you the information to make your own informed decision, not a directive. Estimates are based on recent market data, not a guarantee of what you'll actually pay in fees or postage, or what an item will eventually sell for.

Do I need an account?

Yes — a free account keeps your scan history, offers, and tracked resales tied to you. Every new account starts with a 7-day free trial.

When does the app launch?

Bargin is in final testing now. Join the waitlist and you'll get an email the moment it's live on the App Store and Google Play.

Where does the pricing data come from?

Real, recent completed sales — not asking prices, and not a brand's official RRP. When data is thin for an item, Bargin says so honestly rather than guessing.
