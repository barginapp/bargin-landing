How it works

Two taps from scrolling to a verdict.

No copying links or typing. Bargin sits in the share menu you already use.

1

Share the listing

In Vinted or Depop, tap Share → Bargin. You can also paste a link or upload a screenshot from anywhere else.

2

See the real profit

Bargin matches recent UK sales of similar items and takes off buyer fees, selling fees and postage both ways.

3

Make an offer and track it

Find an offer that still makes money, log it, and Bargin reminds you to check for a reply. Then track it through to sold.

Sold prices, not asking prices

A profit you can actually hit.

Asking prices are wishful. Bargin looks at what similar items really sold for, then prices yours towards the lower-middle of those sales. If they usually go for more, you'll see that too, so you know the upside without being promised it.

FAQ

Good to know

How accurate is the profit?

Bargin prices items cautiously. It looks at what similar items actually sold for in the UK and uses a price towards the lower-middle of those sales, so the profit you see is one you can realistically hit. If items usually sell for more, it shows that as well. It's an estimate, not a guarantee.

Which apps does Bargin work with?

Share any listing straight from Vinted or Depop, paste a link, or upload a screenshot of a listing from anywhere else.

Is Bargin on iPhone?

Bargin is launching on Android first. iPhone is planned next. Join the waitlist and we'll tell you when it's ready.

What does it cost?

Every new account gets a 7-day free trial with up to 15 scans and no payment details. After that, Pro is £8.99 a month for 150 scans and Pro+ is £15.99 a month for 300 scans. One-off scan packs never expire.

Is this financial advice?

No. Bargin gives you the numbers to make your own decision. Estimates are based on recent sales, not a guarantee of what an item will sell for.
