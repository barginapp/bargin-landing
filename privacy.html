Bargin — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Who this covers

This Privacy Policy explains what Bargin collects when you use the app, why, and what your rights are. Bargin is currently run by a single founder (not yet a registered company) and is the "data controller" for the purposes of UK GDPR. If you have questions about your data, contact support@bargin.app.

What we collect

Account details: your email address and password (handled by our authentication provider, Supabase — we never see or store your raw password ourselves). Scan data: the listing URL or details you check, the price, condition, brand and photo of the item, and the verdict, market-price estimate and comp count we calculate for it. Your own activity: offers you log and their outcome, purchases you mark as made, and resale details you enter (sale price, platform, fees, postage). Preferences: your profit/ROI thresholds and fee/postage settings, if you set them. Push notification token, only if you turn on stale-offer nudges. We do not collect payment card details — Bargin has no paid tier yet, and if one launches, payment will be handled by Apple/Google or a payment processor directly, never by us.

How we use it

To run the core feature: turning a listing you paste in into a buy/skip verdict, using real recent sold prices. To track your own offers, purchases and resales so you can see your own history and profit over time. To run your free trial and apply any trial extensions from referrals. To send you a nudge if you opt in to push notifications and an offer has been waiting a few days with no reply. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising — Bargin has no ads and no ad partners.

Who else sees it

A few specialist services process data on our behalf, only for the specific job listed: Supabase (hosts our database and handles sign-in). ScrapingBee (fetches the public page of a listing you paste in, so we can read its price/condition/photos). Google Gemini (helps clean up and categorise listing text). The SoldComps API (looks up real recent sold prices for comparable items). Expo (delivers push notifications, only if you turn them on). None of these providers get your email or account details — only the specific listing/search data each one needs to do its job. We do not otherwise share your data with third parties, and we never sell it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and scan history for as long as your account exists, so your tracking and history stay intact. If you want your account and data deleted, use the "Delete my account" option in the app (Account tab), or email support@bargin.app.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to: tell you what data we hold on you, correct anything inaccurate, delete your data, or give you a copy in a portable format (the app's "Export my data" option does this directly). Email support@bargin.app for any of these and we'll action it as quickly as we reasonably can. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) if you think we've handled your data wrongly.

A note on selling and HMRC

Since January 2024, UK online marketplaces are required to report frequent sellers' income to HMRC. If you use Bargin to buy and resell items regularly, you may need to declare that income yourself — this app doesn't report anything to HMRC on your behalf, and nothing here is tax advice; check gov.uk or a real accountant if you're unsure.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if what we collect or how we use it changes, and update the date above.
