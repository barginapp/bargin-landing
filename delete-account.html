Bargin · Privacy Policy

Delete your Bargin account

You can delete your account and data at any time.

In the app

Open Bargin, go to the Account tab, and tap Delete my account. Your account, scans, offers, stock and sales records are erased from our live systems straight away, and from backups within 30 days.

By email

If you can't open the app, email support@bargin.app from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We'll confirm within one month.

Delete some of your data (keep your account)

In the app you can delete individual scans from History, and individual deals and stock items from Deals → Stock & sales. Deleted items are erased from our live systems straight away and from backups within 30 days.

You can also turn off usage stats in Account → Share usage stats, or email support@bargin.app from the address you signed up with and tell us what you'd like removed. We'll confirm within one month.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Google Play or App Store subscription. Cancel it in your store's subscription settings first. Records Google Play, Apple or RevenueCat must keep for tax and accounting are kept by them under their own policies.
